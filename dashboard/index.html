Class presence, without the roll call.

The extension reads Google Meet participant tiles and reports who is present and whether their mic is on. Students see their own status; professors see the whole class.

Get started Open professor view

1. Extension

Load the unpacked extension in Chrome, then join a Meet. It scans participant tiles and only sends a snapshot when something changes.

2. Backend

Flask accepts snapshots at /api/presence and stores them in SQLite. No external database or hosted service is required.

3. Dashboard

Students check their own status; professors watch the class table refresh every five seconds and spot muted participants instantly.
