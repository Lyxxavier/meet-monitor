This dashboard shows attendance information collected for a Google Meet meeting, including participant names and identifiers, microphone-on or muted status, and timestamps. Audio and video are not recorded by Meet Monitor. Unexported records are retained until the professor exports them; exported records are automatically deleted after 15 days.
